Privacy Policy

Monad – Proper British Gifts · Last updated 10 November 2026

This policy explains how Monad – Proper British Gifts ("we") handles personal data. Contact: monadbarbello@gmail.com.

1. Shop customers

When you order, our shop platform (Shopify) and print partner (Printify) process your name, address, email and order details to fulfil your order and meet legal obligations. Payments are handled by the payment provider; we never see full card details.

2. Our social media app (including TikTok)

Our app connects only to Monad's own TikTok account, via TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API. It uses basic account information (account name and ID) to confirm the connected account and the permission to publish videos and photos that we create. We do not collect, sell or share data about other TikTok users. Access tokens are stored securely in our automation platform and can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings.

3. Legal basis

We process data to perform contracts (your orders), for our legitimate interests (running and promoting our shop), and where required by law.

4. Retention

Order records are kept as long as UK law requires (normally 6 years). App connection data is deleted when the connection is removed.

5. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to processing, by emailing us. You can also complain to the ICO (ico.org.uk).

6. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
